NASA (National Aeronautics and Space Administration) was established on October 1, 1958, by the National Aeronautics and Space Act signed by President Dwight D. Eisenhower. NASA succeeded the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA).

The Apollo program was NASA's third human spaceflight program. Apollo 11 successfully landed astronauts Neil Armstrong and Edwin "Buzz" Aldrin on the Moon on July 20, 1969. Armstrong became the first human to walk on the Moon. Michael Collins orbited the Moon in the Command Module during the mission.

NASA's Space Shuttle program operated from 1981 to 2011. The first Space Shuttle mission, STS-1, was launched on April 12, 1981, aboard Columbia. The program carried 355 unique individuals into space over 135 missions.

The Shuttle Challenger disaster occurred on January 28, 1986, when the orbiter broke apart 73 seconds into its flight due to the failure of an O-ring seal in its right solid rocket booster. All seven crew members were killed.

The Columbia disaster occurred on February 1, 2003, when the orbiter disintegrated during re-entry due to damage to the thermal protection system sustained during launch. All seven crew members aboard were killed.

NASA's Mars exploration program includes the Mars Science Laboratory mission (Curiosity rover), which landed on Mars on August 6, 2012. Curiosity has been actively roving since then. The Perseverance rover landed on Mars on February 18, 2021, and is tasked with astrobiology research.

Hubble Space Telescope was launched on April 24, 1990. It orbits Earth at approximately 547 kilometers above the surface. Hubble has contributed to significant discoveries including determining the age of the universe at approximately 13.8 billion years.

James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) was launched on December 25, 2021. It is a successor to Hubble and observes primarily in the infrared spectrum. JWST is positioned at the second Lagrange point (L2), approximately 1.5 million kilometers from Earth.

NASA's Artemis program aims to return humans to the Moon, including the first woman and first person of color, by the mid-2020s. The Artemis I mission (uncrewed) launched on November 16, 2022.
