Tesla Motors was founded on July 1, 2003, by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. The company is named after inventor and electrical engineer Nikola Tesla. Elon Musk, JB Straubel, and Ian Wright also joined as co-founders in early stages.

Elon Musk served as chairman of Tesla's board of directors from 2004. He became CEO in 2008, replacing Ze'ev Drori. Musk led the development of the Tesla Roadster, the company's first production vehicle, which debuted in 2008.

Tesla's mission is to accelerate the world's transition to sustainable energy. The company produces electric vehicles, battery energy storage systems, solar panels, and related products.

The Tesla Model S, introduced in 2012, became the world's best-selling electric vehicle by mid-2015. The Model 3, released in 2017, was designed as a more affordable mass-market vehicle.

Tesla's Gigafactory 1 in Nevada began operations in 2016. The facility produces lithium-ion battery cells in partnership with Panasonic. As of 2023, Tesla operates multiple Gigafactories across the United States, Europe, and China.

Martin Eberhard served as CEO of Tesla from 2004 until 2007, when he left the company. Marc Tarpenning served as Vice President of Electrical Engineering and was also a founder who departed before the company's major growth phase.

Tesla became the first American automaker to go public since Ford Motor Company in 1956, trading on the NASDAQ under the symbol TSLA. Tesla's stock price rose significantly in 2020, making Elon Musk one of the wealthiest people in the world.
